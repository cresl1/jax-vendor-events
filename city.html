Vendor signup with the city & county

Official applications, permits, fees and simple steps for selling at city and county events in Northeast Florida. Every link goes to the official page. Checked Oct 25, 2026.

14 city & county events on the calendarSee them all with a "How to sign up" button on each.

What every vendor needs first

  • Florida sales tax registration + Annual Resale CertificateEveryone who sells goodsRegister with the Florida Department of Revenue to collect sales tax; registered businesses get an Annual Resale Certificate. Many events ask for your certificate number. Official page
  • Local business tax receipt (LBTR)Most businesses, including home-basedGet it from the tax collector in the county where your business is based. In Duval, receipts run Oct 1 - Sep 30; late renewals add 10% in October plus 5% a month (max 25%). Official page
  • Certificate of insurance (COI)When the event or city asksSome events ask for general liability insurance naming them as additional insured. The City of Jacksonville wants food vendors' COI within 14 days of acceptance. Official page
  • Temporary food event license (DBPR)Food vendors onlyFood vendors without an annual license need a temporary event license from Florida DBPR; it is not needed for jewelry, art or other merchandise. Official page

City of Jacksonville

City of Jacksonville — Office of Special Events · Duval County Checked Oct 25

When we checked on Oct 25 the page said "No Upcoming Vending Opportunities". Applications open event by event (Holiday River Fest, World of Nations, Jazz Fest and more).

City of Jacksonville vendor opportunities
  • Jacksonville Holiday River FestSat, Nov 28 · Jacksonville Verified Oct 25 · How to sign up
  • World of Nations Celebration 2027Annual (2026 edition was Feb 28 - Mar 1). 2027 dates not posted yet · Jacksonville Unverified · How to sign up
  1. Open the Become a Vendor page and check "Current Vending Opportunities".
  2. Tap "receive vending opportunity updates" so you hear when an event opens.
  3. When your event opens, fill in its application during the application window.
  4. If accepted, have your sales tax certificate and business tax receipt ready, plus insurance if the event asks.
  5. Pay the vendor fee the way the city asks (cashier's check or money order).
Fees
Set per event, typically 15% of gross sales. For non-food-truck vendors the fee includes a tent, two tables, two chairs and a banner. Paid by cashier's check or money order to the City of Jacksonville. No refunds.
Insurance
Food vendors send a certificate of insurance within 14 days of being accepted (City general insurance requirements). Insurance requirements
Food vendors
Food vendors: Florida DBPR temporary event license (sold on site) and a JFRD fire inspection permit.

City of St. Augustine

City of St. Augustine — Mobile Vendor / Business Permits · St. Johns County Checked Oct 25

Selling on city streets needs a mobile vendor permit, applied for in person. Vending is restricted in some areas during Nights of Lights (Nov 21, 2026 - Jan 18, 2027).

St. Augustine mobile vendor permit
  1. Read the Mobile Vendor page and the map of prohibited areas.
  2. Fill in the mobile vendor application.
  3. Turn it in in person at 151 King Street (call first).
  4. For a booth at a festival, apply to that event's organizer instead.
